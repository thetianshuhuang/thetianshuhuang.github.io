Tianshu Huang / Photography

I'm an amateur photographer who mostly takes travel photos; recently, I've also been dabbling in events and other research-related work. I started (somewhat) seriously trying to learn photography in 2022; this page documents my best work so far.

Equipment

I started on the Sony E (APS-C) system, mostly using the Sony 18-135mm, 55-210mm, and Sigma 30mm f/1.4 after upgrading from the kit pancake lens. In early 2025, I upgraded to full frame (A7Cii) with roughly equivalent lenses — the Tamron 28-200 and Sony 35mm GM. Some photos also feature borrowed equipment.
